SLS Part Weight Estimator

Estimate mass from the actual solid CAD volume. For hollow or lattice designs, use the material volume after subtracting voids, rather than the bounding-box volume.

Enter volume and density to calculate.

*Preset densities were supplied by TPM3D/Paco Deng and recorded on 3 October 2026; a current public TDS confirming these figures was not obtained in this release. Confirm the exact grade and density before production. These presets are not generic values for every PA12 or glass-filled nylon.

Mass (g) = volume (cm³) × density (g/cm³). 1 cm³ = 1,000 mm³. This estimates material mass only; actual printed mass can differ. It excludes coating, inserts, residual powder, packaging and shipping. It does not calculate manufacturing price, dimensional capability or a delivery date. No CAD or data is uploaded.
