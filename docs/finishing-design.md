# SLS finishing and design handoff

Use this as an RFQ checklist, then agree project-specific acceptance criteria with the supplier.

## CAD and manufacturing inputs

- Supply STEP/STL and a drawing marking critical interfaces and tolerances.
- State whether hollowing or lattice design is already complete. Mark regions where changes are permitted.
- For cavities, identify powder-removal access and how cleanliness will be inspected.
- Mark thin walls, long spans, flex zones and features at risk during handling.
- Explain service loads, fastening, temperature and chemical exposure.
- Provide quantities and whether parts are prototypes or intended production items.

## Surface specification

| Finish question | Information to provide |
|---|---|
| Touch / appearance | Reference sample, photographs and acceptance criteria |
| Color | Pantone reference and approved physical sample where needed |
| Smoothing | Required texture plus dimensions that must be preserved |
| Painting / coating | Color, gloss, adhesion and exposure requirements |
| Assembly | Inserts, mating surfaces and fit test |
| Contact / regulated use | Contact scenario, duration and required evidence |

Review the compatibility of the exact grade, geometry and finishing route. Smoothing is not itself proof of leak tightness, chemical resistance or biological suitability. Test the final finished state when the requirement depends on that state.

## Before production

Agree the material designation, approved finish, inspection method, permitted deviations and sample acceptance. Request a separate engineering scope when lattice design or repeated CAD modifications are needed.

Source: [TPM3D SLS services](https://www.sls-3d.com/services). This is a proposed handoff workflow, not a universal dimensional or material specification.
